Get The Plot, in one page.

A free daily briefing on Portuguese real estate — the deals, developments, people, architecture and rules shaping the market — written by the team at Verde 2050.

Every morning, we read the Portuguese and international press and distil what matters into a single, fast briefing — so the people who build, buy and finance Portugal don't have to.

What it is

Get The Plot is Verde 2050's daily editorial briefing on Portuguese real estate. Each edition spans six beats — deals and transactions, developments and projects, people and moves, architecture and design, rules and regulation, and market and macro — with a one-line read on why each item matters for capital. It lands in your inbox by 8am, every weekday morning, free.

Who writes it

Get The Plot is curated by Verde 2050 — a Portugal-focused living investment platform building places for better living across Lisbon, Cascais and Comporta. We read this market every day for our own work; Get The Plot is what we wished existed, published openly.

Signal, not noise — the deals, buildings, people and rules that actually move the market.

Editorial standards

We source from the Portuguese trade titles and nationals, international agency research, and the official gazette — cross-checking rule changes against primary sources. We publish no advertising and take no sponsorships; nothing in an edition is paid placement. Figures are drawn from public and trade reporting and should be verified before any decision. Get The Plot is information, not investment advice.

Who reads it

Developers, investors, family offices, architects, planners, landowners and agents across Portugal — from Lisbon and Cascais to Comporta, Porto and the Algarve.
